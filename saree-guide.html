A PRACTICAL STARTING POINT

Get to know
the weave.

The name of a saree category can tell you something about its tradition and character. The exact feel still depends on the fabric, weave, finish and individual piece.

01 · SOUTH INDIAN SILK

Kanjivaram

Known for its silk body, strong colours and often-contrasting borders. Traditional designs may include temple-inspired motifs and zari details. It is a popular choice for weddings and major celebrations.

02 · NORTH INDIAN BROCADE

Banarasi

Associated with Varanasi and richly patterned brocade work. Floral and ornamental motifs, detailed borders and metallic threadwork are common features in many Banarasi styles.

03 · SOFTER SILK DRAPES

Soft silk

“Soft silk” is a broad retail description rather than one single weave. It usually points to a silk saree chosen for a softer hand-feel and easier drape; check the specific fabric and care details for each piece.

04 · EVERYDAY FAVOURITE

Cotton

Cotton sarees are valued for their breathable feel and practical wear. Their texture and weight vary by weave and finish, so a light cotton and a more substantial handloom cotton can feel quite different.

Before you choose: ask about the exact fabric composition, blouse piece, length, care instructions and current availability. Category names alone do not confirm whether a saree is pure silk, handwoven or zari-rich.
